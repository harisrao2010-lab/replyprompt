A few clear answers

Frequently asked questions

Does ReplyPrompt generate the final AI answer?

No. This page builds a prompt for you to paste into an AI chat service such as ChatGPT or Gemini. That service generates the reply. Review its answer before sending.

Which channels can I use it for?

Choose WhatsApp, Instagram DM, email, marketplace messages, public review responses, website live chat, or another support channel. The prompt includes your selection as context for the draft.

Which languages are available?

You can request English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Urdu script, Roman Urdu, Indonesian, Turkish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Swahili, or ask the AI to match the customer’s language. The quality depends on the AI service you use, so review translated replies.

Is this customer reply prompt builder free and private?

This static prompt builder requires no sign-up, API key, or payment. It does not send form content from this page to a ReplyPrompt server. If you copy a prompt into another AI service, that service handles the text under its own terms and privacy policy.

Will the AI invent a price, delivery time, or refund policy?

The prompt asks the AI to use only the business facts you provide and to ask for missing details instead of inventing them. AI can still make mistakes, so verify the draft against your actual records and policies before sending.

Does this tool send messages to customers?

No. It does not connect to messaging accounts, access contacts, send messages, or automate replies. You decide what to copy, review, and send.
